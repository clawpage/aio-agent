Accounts and access

Clawpage Agent provides AI task, task browser and workspace services. While there are fewer than 30 accounts in total, you can register without an invitation code; once the total reaches 30, a valid invitation code is required. Protect your account credentials and do not use another person's account. Members access their own tasks and workspace; deployment administration features are used by the administrator.

Content and acceptable use

Submit only content that you are entitled to use and that the task needs. Do not use the service for illegal activity, to violate the privacy or rights of others, for fraud or harassment, to spread hate, or to encourage violence or self-harm. Material a task needs may be sent to the configured AI services and to the websites you ask to use; see the privacy policy for how it is handled.

AI results and service conditions

AI replies may be inaccurate; verify important results. Clawpage Agent is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. For medical, legal and investment questions, consult qualified professionals. The app requires an internet connection, and what the service can do depends on its actual configuration and on the availability of the related services.

Your instructions and responsibility

The AI works on your instructions. What it produces and does under your account, including messages and posts it sends, sign-ins, purchases, code it pushes or deploys, and scheduled tasks that run while you are away, follows the tasks, authorizations and settings you give it, and you are responsible for it. Review results before relying on or sharing them, give it only the access a task needs, and make sure your use follows the law and the terms of the third-party services and accounts you have it use. You can stop a task or delete a scheduled task at any time.

Feedback, privacy and deletion

You can report replies in the app, or request deletion of your account and associated data through “Account & privacy” or the public support form. The administrator handles requests after verifying your identity. Contact email: feedback@clawpage.ai.
