Your AI workspace

一站 · AIO Agent 由 KevinDSoon 提供。将任务写在主会话里，查看 AI 的处理进展和结果，再继续补充要求。任务浏览器允许你查看正在使用的网页并接管操作；工作空间保存任务附件和生成的文件。语音输入把你主动录制的语音转换为待确认的文字。

AIO Agent lets you submit tasks, follow progress, review results and continue the same task. Inspect or take over the task browser, view workspace files, and dictate a message draft. Each member has an isolated task history and workspace.

注册与登录 / Sign up and sign in

使用账号密码或 Google 登录。首次注册需要管理员提供的一次性邀请码；已有账号不需要重新注册。应用需要联网，任务执行依赖服务端及配置的 AI 服务。

Sign in with a username and password or Google. First-time registration requires an invitation code from the administrator. Existing accounts can sign in directly. An internet connection and the configured AI services are required.

使用与支持 / Use and support

AI 回复可能有误，仅供一般参考。医疗、法律和投资问题请咨询合格专业人员。你可在应用内举报回复，也可申请删除账户及关联数据。支持邮箱：feedback@clawpage.ai。

AI results may be inaccurate; verify important results and seek qualified professional advice where needed. Report a response in the app, or request deletion of your account and associated data. Support: feedback@clawpage.ai.
